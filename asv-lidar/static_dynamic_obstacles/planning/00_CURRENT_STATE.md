## 1. The paper in brief

A model-scale autonomous surface vessel (1.725 × 0.50 m) follows a planned path through confined
water, a 10 × 25 m basin and parallel-walled channels. It avoids static panels and one moving
target vessel in a COLREGs-aware way, using onboard perception: a 360° 2D LiDAR, a target tracker
and a map of the navigable boundary.

One frozen problem formulation, **baseline-v3** (`configs/baseline_v3.json`, formulation digest
`03a9c0c22f53ee0b`), is trained with four deep RL learners: PPO, RecurrentPPO, SAC and TQC, three
seeds each. They are evaluated on **test set v4** (1,000 held-out episodes: 664 decoupled, 336
coupled) against classical comparators (LOS-PID + DWA; the encounter-specific VO).

The paper develops the formulation and deploys one policy, the better of SAC and TQC. The other
learners and the classical methods are baselines. Field trials of the deployed policy are part of
the paper.

## 4. Decisions in force

- **Framing (2026-10-10):**
  - The paper presents a formulation and a deployed policy; it is not a learner-comparison study.
  - "LiDAR" stays out of the title and the contribution names; the formulation is stated for
    onboard range sensing.
- **Scope (2026-10-14):**
  - The channel-width sweep (Study 1) and the perception-degradation study (Study 2) are not part
    of the paper. Their claims C-2, C-3 and C-5, tables R4 and R5, and RQ3 and RQ6 are dropped.
  - The width thresholds stay as design reasoning (Section 2.2.4).
  - Field trials are in the paper (S13(a)).
- **COLREGs results (2026-10-08):**
  - Behavior is shown by trajectory snapshots of each encounter type, each checked against
    track-based rule conditions. There is no per-rule compliance table (R2 is dropped).
  - The behavior is "COLREGs-aware", never "COLREGs-compliant", and each rule is stated as exactly
    what the reward encodes.
- **Narrow-channel convention (A17):** the own ship gives way to a crossing target from either
  side, passing astern.
- **Terminology (2026-10-07/08):**
  - *decoupled* / *coupled* scenarios (never "field-style" or "frozen-like");
  - the *validation set* (never "development set");
  - *required turn sense*; *rule-consistent* (never "lawful");
  - American spelling.
- **Seeds and selection:**
  - three seeds per learner, every seed reported, re-trained only for a documented defect (S16);
  - headline at the best validation-set checkpoint, with the final 3.0 M model reported beside it
    (S15);
  - the deployed learner chosen on test set v4 (see section 3).
- **Comparators (S18, A35, 2026-10-20):**
  - The encounter-specific VO (an interpretation of Thyri and Breivik 2022, IFAC-PapersOnLine
    55(31):37–43) replaces COLREGs-VO as the velocity-obstacle comparator. It reads the
    formulation's own encounter assessment.
  - Its passing side is judged on the encounter while engaged.
  - COLREGs-VO remains only as the rule the test set's reactive target follows.
- **Algorithm boxes (S17):** Algorithms 1–4 in the main text; A1 (encounter-specific VO), A2
  (LOS-DWA) and A3 (safety layer, pending) in an appendix.
- **Safety layer:** off in training and in every validation-set evaluation. Learned behavior is
  always reported with it off, and interventions are never credited to the policy.
- **Numbers:** results come only from the results files. The explainer artifact never names a
  field-deployment test set.

## 5. Corrections made 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-24

These are facts that earlier drafts or planning files had wrong; the current files are corrected.

- **Tracker:** the Kalman measurement is the plain cluster centroid (the hull-fit offset exists
  but is off, F64).
- **Track deletion:** a track is dropped after more than 3 consecutive missed updates; it coasts
  for up to 1.5 s.
- **Learner settings** are the Stable-Baselines3 defaults with listed exceptions, not the original
  papers' settings:
  - the common discount 0.951;
  - PPO: 512-step rollouts per simulator, minibatch 512, target KL 0.03;
  - SAC and TQC: 10⁴ random warm-up steps, and one gradient step per collected transition
    (`gradient_steps = 10` with 10 simulators; the library default of 1 per vectorized step would
    be one per 10 transitions);
  - PPO and RecurrentPPO take up to 100 gradient steps per 5,120-step update (10 epochs of 10
    minibatches); the KL limit ends some updates sooner;
  - TQC keeps the library's 2 critics, where its paper used 5.
- **COLREGs-VO head-on test:** |α| ≤ 22.5° and |β| ≤ 22.5°, with β the own ship's bearing seen
  from the target's heading, so each vessel lies within 22.5° of the other's bow. It is not a test
  on the headings (`src/classical/colregs_vo.py`).
- **The reactive target** runs COLREGs-VO with six departures from its settings
  (`MASTER_ARTIFACT_FACTS.md` section 2.3 item 9):
  - it changes course only;
  - it keeps its initial course as the preferred course;
  - the side rule is judged on the encounter;
  - its hard gap is 0.75 m;
  - it ignores static panels;
  - it turns at 8°/s.
- **Pooled table in the results files:** 30 of its 300 cells (`master_artifact_results.json` and
  `MASTER_ARTIFACT_RESULTS.md` section 13, generated 2026-10-22) are off by 0.001 from double
  rounding. An example is LOS-PID + DWA decoupled success, 0.871 where 578/664 = 0.870.
  `results/baseline_v3/paper_table.md` is correct. The generator now keeps six decimals, and the
  next regeneration corrects the results files.
- **The encounter-specific VO** reads the encounter context; LOS-DWA does not.
- **Test set v4 crossings:** 166 from port and 135 from starboard, not balanced.
- **Test set v4 coupled part:** 228 episodes drawn in the three motifs and 108 on three fixed
  reference layouts. Every one has three panels, but 30 have no target (27 motif, 3 reference);
  of the 306 with a target, 156 hold constant speed and 150 change speed once
  (`results/test_set/v4/definition.csv`).
- **Reference-layout episodes** are drawn by the same generator as every other episode, with the
  layout's published leg and three panels passed in as fixed (`src/paper2_set.py`, `_flags`;
  `tools/tiers/test_set.py`, `_layout_episode`). Their generation does not apply the space-time
  check; like every test episode, they pass the test set's feasibility check. Algorithm 4 has a
  fixed-layout branch for them (`PAPER3_ALGORITHMS.md`).
- **Learner libraries:** RecurrentPPO and TQC come from sb3-contrib, PPO and SAC from
  Stable-Baselines3.
- **Metrics not computed by any evaluation:** the maximum cross-track error and the perception
  metrics (acquisition range, classification latency, velocity error, occlusion duration).
- **Test-set channels:** recorded nominal widths 6.9–10.6 m (median 8.7 m), narrowing to about
  5.6–6.2 m at the CPA in the narrowest cases. They lie entirely above the head-on (3.80 m) and
  overtaking (4.26 m) widths and mostly above the 6.30 m centerline head-on width. The crossings
  span the 7.60 m crossing width.
- **The validation set** has no digest of its own; it is regenerated from its seed blocks by the
  frozen generator (formulation digest 03a9c0c22f53ee0b).
- **References still to confirm:** Fossen (2021), publisher location; Fossen, Breivik and Skjetne
  (2003), IFAC Proc. Vol. 36(21), 211–216.
